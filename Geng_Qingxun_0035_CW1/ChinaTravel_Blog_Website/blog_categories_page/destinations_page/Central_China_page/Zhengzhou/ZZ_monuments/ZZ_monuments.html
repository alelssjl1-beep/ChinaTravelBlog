Top 3 Historic Sites in Zhengzhou

Erlitou Relic Museum

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Erlitou Relic Museum is the "cradle of Chinese dynastic civilization"—the ruins of the late Xia Dynasty (c. 1900–1500 BCE), the earliest confirmed state-level capital in China. Discovered in the 1950s, this site rewrote China’s archaeological history, proving the Xia Dynasty wasn’t just a myth but a real civilization with advanced urban planning.The museum spans 300,000 square meters, with two main areas: the outdoor ruin site and the indoor exhibition hall. Outside, you can walk along the remains of the Xia Dynasty’s palace complex (including a 10,000-square-meter main palace), city walls, and sacrificial pits—imagine kings holding rituals here 4,000 years ago. Inside, exhibits include stunning jade artifacts (like a 70cm-long jade cong, a ritual object), bronze wine vessels (the earliest known Chinese bronzes), and pottery tools that show daily life in the Xia Dynasty.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–April): Mild weather, and the museum’s courtyard has blooming peonies.

Weekdays: Fewer tourists than weekends—better for listening to guides.

Introduction

Shaolin Temple, founded in 495 CE during the Northern Wei Dynasty, is world-famous as the birthplace of Shaolin Kung Fu and Chan (Zen) Buddhism. Nestled at the foot of Songshan Mountain, it’s a serene complex of red temples, ancient pines, and stone pagodas—yet it’s also alive with the energy of monks practicing martial arts.

Best Time to Visit

Autumn (October–November): Cool weather, and Songshan Mountain’s leaves turn golden—beautiful for photos.

Weekdays: Avoid weekend crowds (tour buses arrive in droves); go on a Tuesday or Wednesday.

Price

Entry Fee: 80 CNY/adult; 40 CNY/students (includes entry to Songshan Mountain and Pagoda Forest).

Kung Fu Show: Included in the entry fee (no extra charge).

Zhengzhou Shang City Ruins Park & Zhengzhou Museum

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Zhengzhou Shang City Ruins Park preserves the remains of the Shang Dynasty’s capital (c. 1600–1046 BCE)—a 3,600-year-old city that was once the political and cultural center of China. The park spans 25 square kilometers, with the most impressive feature being the ancient city wall: 10 meters tall, 20 meters wide, and stretching 7 kilometers—you can walk along parts of it and touch the rammed earth that has stood for millennia.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April–May): The park’s cherry trees bloom, and the museum’s indoor exhibits are cool.

Evening (Park Only): The park stays open till 10:00 PM—walk along the lit-up city wall for a romantic, quiet experience.

Price

Zhengzhou Shang City Ruins Park: Free entry.

Zhengzhou Museum: Free entry (book tickets online 1 day in advance via the museum’s WeChat account).
