Top 3 Nature Spots in Guilin

Li River Scenic Area

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

The Li River, Guilin’s most iconic natural attraction, winds 83 kilometers from Guilin to Yangshuo, flanked by jagged karst peaks, green rice paddies, and bamboo groves. The most famous stretch (between Xingping and Yangshuo) features peaks like “Elephant Trunk Hill” (shaped like an elephant drinking water) and “Yellow Cloth Shoal” (where the river’s water turns emerald green). The best way to experience it is by a 4-hour cruise: you’ll glide past mist-shrouded peaks at sunrise, watch fishermen use cormorants to catch fish, and see locals working in rice paddies along the banks. For a more intimate experience, rent a bamboo raft (short 1-hour trips) near Yangshuo.

Best Time to Visit

Early Morning (7:00 AM–9:00 AM): Misty peaks, soft light, and fewer crowds.

Spring (March–April) or Autumn (September–November): Clear water, mild weather (15°C–25°C).

Price

4-Hour Guilin-to-Yangshuo Cruise: 210 CNY/adult; 105 CNY/children (1.2m–1.4m); free for kids under 1.2m.

Bamboo Raft (1-hour trip, Yangshuo section): 80–100 CNY/person (negotiable with vendors).

Yangshuo West Street & Surrounding Karst

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Yangshuo, a small town 65 kilometers from Guilin, is surrounded by some of the most dramatic karst scenery in China. While West Street (Yangshuo’s main pedestrian street) is busy with shops and cafes, the real magic is just outside town: trails wind through karst peaks like “Moon Hill” (a peak with a circular hole that looks like a moon) and “Butterfly Spring” (a valley with waterfalls and wildflowers). You can hike to the top of Moon Hill for panoramic views of Yangshuo’s karst landscape, or rent an electric bike (50 CNY/day) to explore the countryside—passing rice paddies, ancient villages, and quiet rivers. At sunset, head to “West Street Beach” to watch the sky turn pink over the peaks.

Best Time to Visit

Afternoon (2:00 PM–5:00 PM): Cooler for hiking; golden light for photos.

Autumn (October–November): Rice paddies turn golden, adding color to the landscape.

Price

Moon Hill Entry Fee: 35 CNY/adult; 17 CNY/students.

Electric Bike Rental: 50 CNY/day (includes a helmet and lock).

Butterfly Spring Entry Fee: 60 CNY/adult; 30 CNY/students.

Reed Flute Cave (Ludi Yan)

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Reed Flute Cave, 5 kilometers northwest of Guilin’s downtown, is a 240-million-year-old limestone cave filled with stalactites, stalagmites, and stone pillars—all lit up with colorful LED lights (red, blue, green) that make the cave look like a “Crystal Palace.” The cave’s name comes from the reeds growing outside its entrance (locals once used them to make flutes). Inside, you’ll walk along a 500-meter path past formations with whimsical names like “Dragon Palace” (a large chamber with a stone “dragon”) and “Peach Blossom Mountain” (a cluster of pink-lit stalactites). The cave stays cool (18°C–20°C year-round), making it a great escape from Guilin’s summer heat.

Best Time to Visit

Weekdays (10:00 AM–11:00 AM) or (2:00 PM–3:00 PM): Fewer tour groups than weekends.

Year-round: Indoor, so it’s comfortable in all seasons.

Price

Entry Fee: 90 CNY/adult; 45 CNY/students (with ID); free for kids under 1.2m.

Audio Guide: 20 CNY/rental (available in English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean).
