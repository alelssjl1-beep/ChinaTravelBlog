Top 3 Historic Sites in Beijing

The Forbidden City (Palace Museum)

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

The Forbidden City, in central Beijing, was the imperial palace for 24 Ming and Qing emperors (1406–1912). Covering 720,000㎡ (100 football fields), it’s the world’s largest preserved ancient royal wooden complex and a UNESCO site. Divided into “Outer Court” (ceremonies) and “Inner Court” (living areas), key spots include the Hall of Supreme Harmony (coronations) and the Palace of Heavenly Purity (emperor’s bedroom). It holds 1.8 million relics like porcelain and jade.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April–May): Mild (15–22°C) with blooming gardens.

Autumn (Sept–Oct): Golden light enhances red walls/golden roofs.

Arrive 8:30–10 AM to avoid crowds; skip summer (hot/crowded) and winter (cold).

Price

Peak (April–Oct): 60 CNY/adult, free <1.4m kids, 30 CNY students.

Off (Nov–March): 40 CNY/adult, 20 CNY students.

Online only (book 7 days in advance via official site/WeChat—sells out fast!).

Badaling Great Wall

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Badaling, 70km northwest of Beijing, is the most accessible Great Wall section and a UNESCO site. Built in the Ming Dynasty, it’s 7.8km long with 19 watchtowers. Its stone steps and crenellated walls offer sweeping mountain views—you can walk 1–2 hours to explore key towers like the “No. 8 Tower” (highest point). It’s perfect for first-timers, with facilities like rest areas and souvenir shops.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April–May): Cool (10–18°C), green mountains.

Autumn (Sept–Oct): Crisp (12–20°C), clear views.

Skip weekends/holidays (overcrowded); visit 9–11 AM or 2–4 PM.

Price

Peak (April–Oct): 40 CNY/adult, free <1.2m kids, 20 CNY students.

Off (Nov–March): 35 CNY/adult, 17.5 CNY students.

Cable car: 100 CNY/round-trip, 60 CNY/one-way.

Temple of Heaven

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

The Temple of Heaven, in southern Beijing, was where Ming/Qing emperors prayed for good harvests. Covering 2.73㎡, its core is the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests—a circular wooden building (no nails!) with a blue-tiled roof. The Echo Wall (whispers carry across 32m) and the “Triple Sound Stone” (3 echoes when you clap) are fun highlights. It’s quieter than the Forbidden City, great for relaxing walks.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April–May): Peach blossoms bloom, 15–20°C.

Autumn (Sept–Oct): Cool (14–19°C), fewer crowds.

Early morning (6–8 AM) is peaceful; avoid summer rain.

Price

Park entry: 15 CNY (peak), 10 CNY (off).

Hall of Prayer/Echo Wall area: 20 CNY (peak), 15 CNY (off).

Combo (park + key sites): 35 CNY (peak), 25 CNY (off).
