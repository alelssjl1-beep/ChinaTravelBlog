Top 3 Nature Spots in Beijing

Fragrant Hills Park (Xiangshan Park)

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Fragrant Hills Park, 20km northwest of Beijing, is a 160-hectare mountain retreat. Famous for autumn foliage (10,000+ maple trees turn red/gold Oct–Nov), its highest peak (Xianglu Peak, 557m) offers Beijing skyline views. Historic spots include Jianxinzhai (Qing imperial garden) and Lingguang Temple (holds a Buddha tooth relic). Hiking trails range from easy (to gardens) to steep (to the peak).

Best Time to Visit

Autumn (Oct 15–Nov 15): Peak foliage, 10–18°C.

Spring (April–May): Peach blooms, 10–18°C.

Summer (June–Aug): Cool shade (20–28°C); winter (Dec–Feb): Snowy serenity (below 0°C).

Price

Peak (April–Oct): 10 CNY/adult, 5 CNY students, free <1.2m kids.

Off (Nov–March): 5 CNY/adult, 2.5 CNY students.

Cable car: 80 CNY/one-way, free <1.2m kids.

Summer Palace

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

The Summer Palace, 15km northwest of Beijing, is a 290-hectare royal garden (UNESCO site). Kunming Lake (covers 3/4 of the park) is its center—rent a boat (60–120 CNY/h) to explore. Key spots: Long Corridor (728m, 10,000+ paintings), Longevity Hill (panoramic lake views), and Seventeen-Arch Bridge (connects to an island). It’s perfect for a half-day of relaxing or sightseeing.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April–May): Cherry blossoms by the lake, 15–22°C.

Autumn (Sept–Oct): Golden leaves, 14–20°C.

Evening (6–8 PM): Lake lights up—beautiful for photos (open till 8 PM in summer).

Price

Park entry: 30 CNY (peak), 20 CNY (off).

Combo (park + key sites: Long Corridor/Longevity Hill): 60 CNY (peak), 40 CNY (off).

Boat rental: 60–120 CNY/h (depends on boat type).

Shihuadong Cave

Introduction Best Time to Visit Price
Introduction

Shihuadong Cave, 55km southwest of Beijing, is a 2,500m-long underground cave with stalactites and stalagmites (formed over 300 million years). Shapes like “Flower Mountain” and “Dragon Palace” are lit with colored lights, creating a magical vibe. The cave stays 13–18°C year-round, making it a summer escape. A small river runs through parts of the cave—you can take a short boat ride (20 CNY) inside.

Best Time to Visit

Summer (June–Aug): Escape heat (cave is 13–18°C).

Winter (Dec–Feb): Avoid cold outside; cave is warm.

Visit 10 AM–2 PM (fewer crowds); skip rainy days (cave may be more damp).

Price

Cave entry: 70 CNY/adult, 35 CNY students, free <1.2m kids.

Boat ride inside cave: 20 CNY/person (optional).
