Hi, I’m Alessia

Launched in 2025, this China Travel Blog helps travelers explore Mainland China easily.

I focus on it as many overseas friends only know Beijing and Shanghai, missing other great spots. Content comes from my trips or locals’ advice.

The blog is user-friendly: “Destinations” follows 7 Mainland regions; “Heritage”, “Nature”, “Food” cater to interests; “Guide” covers visas, packing. Later, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan content will show China’s diversity.

More than a guide, it’s a bridge to feel Mainland China’s warmth. Contact via “Contact” for questions. Thanks for following—hope it makes your trip great.
