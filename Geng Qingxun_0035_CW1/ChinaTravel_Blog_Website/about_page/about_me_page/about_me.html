Alessia Geng

Hi there! I’m Alessia, a native Chinese student currently studying abroad—and the person behind this ChinaTravel Blog.

Biography

I grew up in Shanghai, surrounded by China’s rich mix of old and new. As a kid, family weekend trips to nearby towns made me fall in love with Mainland China’s diversity—ancient temples, futuristic skyscrapers, and endless new foods to try.

When studying abroad, I noticed many friends knew little about Mainland China beyond Beijing and Shanghai. I started this blog to share the real Mainland China—the hidden villages, friendly locals, and “wow” foods.

I’m not a professional traveler—just someone who loves their home country and wants to make it easy for you to explore. I share tips (like which Great Wall section to avoid on weekends) and trip stories (like a Yunnan grandma teaching me to make dumplings).

Whether you’re planning a trip or just curious, I hope this blog feels like a friend telling you about their favorite places. Thanks for reading—let’s explore together!
